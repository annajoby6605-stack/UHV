Automated Civic Workflow

How Fill the Pockets Works

A seamless 5-step cycle bridging active rural citizens with Gram Panchayat public works.

01
📸

Capture Road Photo

Villagers take or upload a photograph of the road defect, pothole cavity, or surface damage using mobile camera or gallery.

02
📍

Submit Location

The device captures GPS coordinates automatically, with an easy manual fallback for road name and ward details.

03
🧠

AI Checks Image

Our deep learning vision model analyzes the pixel tensors and asphalt texture gradient. Unrelated images are rejected with zero points.

04
🪙

Eligible Reports Earn Points

Eligible, verified, non-duplicate reports automatically earn exactly 20 points in the citizen's rewards wallet.

05
🚜

Panchayat Repairs Road

Panchayat employees track verified reports on their dashboard, dispatch maintenance crews, and update progress to Resolved.

Community Transformation

Rural Road Repair & Village Improvement

Real-world community impact: empowering rural villages with dependable road connectivity, safer transportation, and cooperative pride.

🌾 🚜 🛣️
Agricultural Logistics

Farmer Market Linkage

Smooth, pothole-free village roads reduce produce spoilage and transport damage for tractors, carts, and small commercial vehicles carrying harvests to regional markets.

✓ Faster delivery & reduced vehicular wear
🚶‍♂️ 🎒 🚸
Safe Village Commutes

Pedestrian & School Bus Safety

Repairing deep waterlogged potholes and cracked road shoulders prevents daily accidents for school children, cyclists, and senior villagers walking between wards.

✓ Waterlogging prevention & pedestrian safety
🏛️ 🛠️ 📋
Public Accountability

Accountable Road Maintenance

Gram Panchayat maintenance crews log physical repair milestones with field timestamps, creating a tamper-proof digital audit trail of village road maintenance.

✓ Institutional transparency & verified repair notes
